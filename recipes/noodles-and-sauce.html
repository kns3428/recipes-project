Noodles and Sauce

Noodles and Sauce is an easy comfort food dish that can be enjoyed by anyone

Ingredients

  • noodles
  • sauce
  • canned black olives
  • turkey pepparoni
  • cheese

Instructions

  1. boil water in a pot
  2. add noodles to boiling water, cooking to packadge instructions
  3. in a separate pot, combine saunce, pepparoni, and olives and let simmer
  4. sprinkle with cheese and serve
