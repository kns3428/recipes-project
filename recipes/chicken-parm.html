Chicken Parm

This fun take on chicken parm is fun for the whole family!

Ingredients

  • chicken nuggets
  • sauce
  • cheese
  • canned or frozen peas

Instructions

  1. cook chicken nuggets per package instructions, but take them out of the oven when they are half done
  2. strinkle each nugget with sauce and cheese
  3. continue cooking
  4. cook peas per package instructions
  5. let cool slightly and serve
